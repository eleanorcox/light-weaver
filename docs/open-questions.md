# Open questions

Decisions that shape the plan. Roughly in order of how much they change it.

1. **Browser or TouchDesigner?** The browser (TypeScript, like the aquarium) can be shared as a link, can be tested, and reuses the aquarium's projector window and corner correction. TouchDesigner is the tool for projection installations and worth learning, but probably needs a Windows or Mac machine (unchecked). Possibly both: the weave core in TypeScript, drawn in either.
2. **Which traditions first?** Proposed: Scottish tartan and a twill or herringbone tweed, because both follow strict, well-documented rules and look very different. Then one with a personal link, such as Dumbara.
3. **Where do the patterns come from?** Published drafts, registers such as the tartan register, museum collections, the Sri Lankan design book? Each source's licence decides what can be used.
4. **How is respect handled for each tradition?** Who decides what is appropriate to reproduce? Reading what makers in each tradition say, contacting them, or only using what is clearly published for reuse?
5. **What does it look like?** How literal the threads are (realistic yarn, or pure lines of light), and how much the cloth fills the frame. Pictures of things Eleanor likes would help, as with the website's moodboards.
6. **Is the name free?** "Lightweaver" was the title of the Make article about Moyer's work and may be associated with him (unchecked). Worth checking before the name is final.
