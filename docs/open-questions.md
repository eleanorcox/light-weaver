# Open questions

Decisions that shape the plan. Roughly in order of how much they change it.

1. **Where do the patterns come from?** First round of research done: see [research/british-traditions.md](research/british-traditions.md). Real tartan thread counts need an account with the Scottish Register of Tartans, and bulk use needs asking; tweed twills can be written from first principles; no Welsh double cloth drafts are published. Contacting the register is Eleanor's call.
2. **Which British tradition first?** Recommended after research: tartan, starting with generated setts (which need no permission) and adding real registered tartans once access is agreed. Then tweed twills and herringbone, not named Harris Tweed.
3. **How is respect handled for each tradition?** Who decides what is appropriate to reproduce? Reading what makers in each tradition say, contacting them, or only using what is clearly published for reuse?
4. **What does it look like?** How literal the threads are (realistic yarn, or pure lines of light), and how much the cloth fills the frame. Pictures of things Eleanor likes would help, as with the website's moodboards.
5. **Is the name free?** "Lightweaver" was the title of the Make article about Moyer's work and may be associated with him (unchecked). Worth checking before the name is final.
6. **Heritage designs that may need permission.** Found in the second round of research and left out until Eleanor has looked into them: the Hudson's Bay Company point-blanket stripe, estate checks such as Coigach and Ardtornish, and whether "Irish Linen" is a protected mark. Details in [research/structures.md](research/structures.md#left-for-eleanor-to-look-into).
