# Research: British weaving traditions

What needs finding out before the first British traditions can go in the catalogue. Nothing here is researched yet: every tradition and fact below is from memory and unchecked.

## Candidates

| Tradition | Where | Kind of pattern | Why it might be first |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tartan | Scotland | Stripe sequence (sett) in 2/2 twill | Strictest rules; a public register |
| Harris Tweed | Outer Hebrides | Loom draft: twills, herringbone | Protected by law, with its own authority |
| Houndstooth, Glen check (Prince of Wales check), gun club check | Scotland, then British suiting | Colour order plus twill | Small repeats; colour-and-weave effects, where the pattern comes from the order of colours as much as the weave |
| Welsh double cloth (carthen, "tapestri") | Wales | Two layers of cloth that swap places | Bold geometric blankets; a different structure from everything else here |
| Welsh flannel and blankets | Wales | Twills and checks | Mill records may survive |
| Witney blankets | Oxfordshire | Twill, with coloured stripes at the ends | Plain, recognisable |
| Paisley shawls | Paisley, Scotland | Jacquard-woven figures | Ties to the punched-card loom; the motif itself came from Kashmir, which needs crediting |
| Spitalfields silk | London | Figured silk on draw looms | Huguenot weavers; museum collections |
| Irish linen damask | Northern Ireland | Damask: satin and its reverse | Shimmering patterns from one colour |
| Axminster and Wilton carpets | Devon, Wiltshire | Woven pile | Probably later: pile is a different structure |

## Questions for each tradition

1. **How is it woven?** Structure, typical threading and treadling or sett, yarns, thread counts.
2. **Where are real patterns published?** Registers, published drafts, pattern books, museum collections, mill archives.
3. **What may be reproduced?** Licence or copyright of each source. Some tartans are owned or restricted to a family, a company or an organisation; "Harris Tweed" is a protected name.
4. **What is its history,** in a few sentences, for the caption?
5. **Who should be credited?**

## Likely sources to check

- The Scottish Register of Tartans (setts and licence terms).
- The Harris Tweed Authority.
- St Fagans National Museum of History and the National Wool Museum, Wales.
- The V&A and National Museums Scotland collections.
- Handweaving.net and published weaving draft collections (for twills and overshot; licence to check).

## How research is done

Following the shared rules: research goes to a subagent with sources required for every fact, and the results come back here, each with its source. Anything without a source stays unchecked.
